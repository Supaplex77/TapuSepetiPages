Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

At Tapu Sepeti ("the App") we care about your privacy. This policy explains plainly what information is processed and where when you use the App.

In short: the App needs no account and collects no personal data. Your saved deeds are stored only on your device. There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The following information is kept only on your device (in the App's local iOS storage) and is never sent to us:

  • The deeds you save (parcel details, boundary coordinates) and the names you give them
  • A cache of parcels you have looked up and of province, district and neighbourhood lists (so reopening them needs no new query)
  • Results of satellite-history scans and your language preference

2. Requests the App Makes

To fetch what you ask for, the App makes only the requests below. They go directly to the respective service, not to us, and contain nothing that identifies you (no name, account or device identifier):

  • TKGM (Turkish Land Registry and Cadastre): for a parcel query, the point you choose (latitude/longitude) or the province/district/neighbourhood and block/parcel number is sent to TKGM's public parcel-query service.
  • Esri World Imagery Wayback: on the Satellite History screen, requests for the parcel's location and map tiles are made to Esri's public imagery archive.
  • Apple (MapKit): maps and satellite imagery, the address you type in address search, and directions are handled by Apple Maps services, under Apple's privacy policy.

These services are subject to their own privacy policies. The App's developer does not see these requests and stores no data on any server.

3. Permissions

  • Location (while using the App): to show where you are on the map and to measure the distance and direction to your parcel. Your location is sent to TKGM only when you look up the parcel at your own position.
  • Camera: only to show your parcel's corner points in augmented reality (AR). Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Motion sensors: to align the AR view and the compass with the direction your phone is facing; processed on your device.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings > Tapu Sepeti.

4. Ads, Analytics and Tracking

The App shows no ads, collects no usage statistics or crash reports, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

5. Deleting Your Data

You can delete a saved deed from its parcel detail screen. Deleting the App from your device removes all local data, including the cache.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. As it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

7. Your Rights (KVKK / GDPR)

Your rights under Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are reserved. Since the developer holds no personal data about you, you stay in full control of your data; for any questions you can reach us at the address below.

8. Important Note

Tapu Sepeti shows public parcel-query data from TKGM and is not affiliated with TKGM or any government agency. Information is for reference only.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.
